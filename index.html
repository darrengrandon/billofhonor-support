Privacy Policy

Bill of Honor respects your privacy. In short: we don't collect anything.

  • Zero personal data collected. Bill of Honor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • Zero ads. The game contains no advertising of any kind.
  • Zero In-App Purchases. There is nothing to buy inside the game.
  • Zero third-party analytics SDKs. No tracking or analytics libraries are included.

Apple Game Center: Leaderboards and achievements are provided through Apple Game Center and are handled entirely by Apple at the OS level. Swift Heron does not receive or store any of that information. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy for details.
